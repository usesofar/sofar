We're building Pantry, a tiny kitchen-inventory CLI. Here's the roadmap so you
have the whole picture. We'll go roughly in this order, one piece per session:

1. Items (today): add and list what's in the pantry.
2. Low marks: a per-item low mark; `list` flags items at or under it.
3. Remove and undo: `remove <item>` is a soft delete; `undo` restores the last
   removal; removed items never list.
4. Shopping list: `shop` prints the items at or under their low mark.
5. Recipes: `recipe add <name> <item>=<qty>,...` and `recipes`.
6. Cook: `cook <name>` subtracts a recipe's quantities, refusing when short.
7. Expiry: `expires <item> <YYYY-MM-DD>`; `expiring` lists items within 3 days
   of PANTRY_TODAY.
8. Report: `report` prints the counts of items, low items and expiring items.
9. Say: `node pantry.js say "<text>"`, a one-line text command box. It
   understands exactly these commands and prints ONE line of JSON,
   {"reply": string, "changed": boolean}:
   - "add <item> x<n>": adds n of the item (creating it); reply
     "added <n> <item>, now <total>"; changed true
   - "how much <item>": reply "<item>: <qty>", or "<item>: none" when the item
     is unknown; changed false
   - "use <item> x<n>": subtracts n, never below 0; reply
     "used <n> <item>, now <total>"; changed true
   - "list": reply the items sorted by name as "<item> <qty>" joined by "; ",
     or "pantry is empty"; changed false
   - "forget <item>": removes the item entirely; reply "forgot <item>",
     changed true; or "nothing called <item>", changed false, when unknown
   Anything else gets the reply
   "commands: add <item> x<n>; how much <item>; use <item> x<n>; list; forget <item>"
   with changed false.

Today: items.

- `node pantry.js add <item> <n>` adds n of an item, creating it or adding to
  its quantity. n is a positive integer; anything else is an error (exit 1,
  message on stderr).
- `node pantry.js list` prints one line per item, "<item> <qty>", sorted by
  name; nothing when the pantry is empty.
- Item names are single lowercase words; the CLI lowercases what it is given.
