The architectural move that makes it work is that the determinism law is only broken when inference runs INSIDE the fold; it is not broken when inference runs outside and its output is recorded as ordinary events, because the fold then remains a pure function of the event set and the non-determinism sits in a producer where it is auditable. The two routes are layers, not alternatives, and both are reachable. The architectural move that makes it work is that the determinism law is only broken when inference runs INSIDE the fold; it is not broken when inference runs outside and its output is recorded as ordinary events, because the fold then remains a pure function of the event set and the non-determinism sits in a producer where it is auditable. That is the same trick sofar already rests on — it never infers, it folds.
